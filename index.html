About

One year in, and I’m already learning how stories move people.

In just one year of editing, I’ve built a sharp eye for pacing, emotion, and attention — turning raw footage into edits that feel intentional from the first frame to the last. I work closely with brands, creators, and storytellers who want their message to land with clarity and impact.

My process is simple: find the feeling, shape the rhythm, and let the story breathe. Whether it’s a commercial, a short film, or a social-first edit, I focus on making every second matter.
